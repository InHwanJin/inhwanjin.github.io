In-Hwan Jin

I'm an M.S. student in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Pusan National University, where I work in the Computer Vision and Signal Processing (CVSP) Laboratory advised by Professor Kyeongbo Kong.

My long-term goal is to develop Spatial Physical Intelligence, enabling AI to understand the physical world and predict its responses to actions. So far, I have worked on 3D/4D scene reconstruction and generation with Gaussian Splatting, from heterogeneous motion reconstruction to interactive environment generation. Building on this, I aim to develop physically grounded 3D/4D representations that integrate physical properties with geometry and motion.

I am expected to graduate with my M.S. in August 2027 and am currently seeking Ph.D. positions.
